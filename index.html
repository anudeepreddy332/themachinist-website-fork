About

I design and build machine learning systems and agentic AI pipelines that convert raw data into decisions, tools, and automated workflows. My foundation is end-to-end ML — data engineering, feature design, model development, and production automation — with a focus on predictive systems, risk modeling, and operational intelligence. Right now I'm building through a structured 5-phase agentic AI curriculum: from raw tool-calling loops (Phase 1) and persistent knowledge retrieval with hybrid RAG (Phase 2), through self-correcting execution agents with LangGraph (Phase 3 — in progress), multi-agent orchestration (Phase 4), and production deployment with FastAPI, LangFuse, and reliability engineering (Phase 5). Each phase ships a real, working system — not a tutorial. I'm interested in systems that anticipate failures, learn from context, and operate reliably in production — whether that's predictive maintenance, autonomous research pipelines, or agents that debug and fix themselves.
